How your marks compare

Each row places you next to the average student in each stream. The closer your dot sits to a colour, the more that subject pulls you towards it.

How it works

  1. You enter four numbers

    Your maths, science and arts marks out of 100, and roughly how many hours you study in a week.

  2. {{ info.trees }} decision trees vote

    Each tree learned from past students which combinations of marks led to which stream. Every tree casts one vote for you.

  3. You see the split, not just a label

    The bar shows how the votes divided. A 95 to 5 split is a clear lean; a 55 to 45 split means both streams are realistic for you.

The model, honestly

Pathway is a {{ info.algorithm }} classifier trained on {{ "{:,}".format(info.records) }} student records. It is a starting point for a conversation with a teacher or parent, not a decision.
